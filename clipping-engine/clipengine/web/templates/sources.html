New recordings from enabled sources are imported, transcribed, clipped and placed in Ready to Review automatically. A source is enabled only if its site is listed in ALLOWED_URL_DOMAINS or you have recorded a written permission for it (Settings → Source permissions). Platforms whose terms forbid automated access (e.g. Rumble, YouTube) stay disabled until the platform's written permission is recorded. Nothing logs in, uses cookies, or bypasses paywalls/DRM. The PC must be on for monitoring to run.
