Automatic clean-up

Deleting a raw recording means its clips can no longer be re-cut from the source. Transcript, captions, timecodes, provenance and finished clips are kept. Your own uploads, inbox files and the {{ legacy }} recording(s) that existed before this feature are never deleted automatically.

Unreviewed and approved-but-unpublished clips are kept; a clip marked Keep never expires.
