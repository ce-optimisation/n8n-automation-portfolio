# Clipping Engine private pilot operator guide

## Local launch and setup

Use a dedicated Windows account for a customer pilot. Keep the release store outside workspace data. Validate the trusted release manifest before activating a release, and keep the previous verified selection available. Use the verified Python and FFmpeg paths with the workspace launcher. The validated workspace descriptor sets a different loopback port and dedicated data and configuration paths for each workspace. Keep the browser on the local address unless a separately approved access arrangement exists.

A fresh customer workspace starts with no owner brands, creators or linked accounts. Open **Creators**, add a brand with an existing niche, and add and assign a creator. Record who authorised source use, the exact permission reference and applicable scope before enabling capture or processing. Confirm any connected account by its stable platform ID. New custom-brand publishing consent is still subject to provider and application eligibility testing.

## Campaigns, review and reporting

Open **Results → Campaign briefs**. Enter a stable reference and the original brief. Supported labelled terms are Campaign, Duration, Source window, Destination and Submission deadline. Revisions get new fingerprints. Review any held rule against the source before using the brief. Matching recordings and clips are review aids. Establish applicant, destination and redistribution eligibility independently.

For an approved clip with rights evidence and the original recording available, queue a private preview at 720p or 1080p. Caption addition respects the existing source-caption flag. The preview worker creates a separate file and can wait while live recording is busy. Verify playback, disclosures, caption timing and destination details before any public approval.

Use structured assessments for estimates. Record the publisher’s URL, campaign disposition and evidence after submission. Record actual receipts by payment reference, currency, gross and fees in minor units. Review totals currency by currency. Absence of receipt evidence means cash is unrecorded. The screens do not submit campaigns, activate advertising, or transfer money.

## Agency and support

Open **Results → Agency prospects** to import verified research or export the local register. Review each prospect’s evidence and suppression state before seeking approval on a specific outreach pilot. Keep signed scopes and rights evidence in the designated client workspace. Review the exported files with the client before delivery.

For a support request, provide the release revision, affected route or job, the time of the failure and short reproduced symptoms. Keep credentials, OAuth tokens and private client material out of tickets. If a new release fails, first stop work in that workspace and preserve its verified SQLite backup. Select the previous verified code in the release store. Confirm schema compatibility before restoring a backed-up database and verify media, account mappings and local settings against the customer’s records.

Caption and title preferences can be exported and restored separately to a new workspace. Full customer media export, deletion, licence enforcement and live payment recovery are outside the verified pilot. Keep commercial charging and distribution disabled while those controls are under development.
